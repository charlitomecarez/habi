## Release notes

This text is a copy of the GitHub release.

This release has three fixes and 23 new features: five Money features, nine new modules and nine changes across the app.

### Fixes

- **Vault:** The vault accepts the correct master password on Android. Old entries still open, and no data changes.
- **Keyboard:** The keyboard no longer covers forms and sheets on Android.
- **Sign-in:** The email sign-in screen takes the 8-digit code.

### Money

- **Daily spending calendar:** A month calendar shows spending and income per day and the month totals. Tap a day to see its transactions.
- **Subscriptions add their expense:** On the billing day, an hourly job adds the expense once and moves the date. This is on for new subscriptions. The reminder stays.
- **Bills with a changing amount:** Bills have their own screen, a due date and a reminder. Pay opens Log payment for the amount.
- **Monthly budgets:** Set a limit per category. Unspent money carries over, and overspending does not. Notifications arrive at 80% and 100%.
- **Savings goals:** A goal counts only the money you mark for it. It shows how much to save each month to reach the date.

### New modules

The new modules are under More, in the sections Daily life, People, Home, Records and Work. The five tabs stay the same.

- **Content planner:** Take each video from idea to published with Next up, Board, Calendar, Ideas and a checklist. Tasks are todos, and publish dates send reminders.
- **Debts (utang):** Track who owes you and who you owe. A payment adds the Money transaction, and the due date sends a reminder.
- **People and dates:** Store birthdays, anniversaries and gift ideas. A reminder arrives one week before, every year. A bought gift adds an expense.
- **Documents and renewals:** Track LTO, license, passport, insurance and PhilHealth. A reminder arrives 30 days before expiry. The Vault key encrypts the photos.
- **Habits:** Each habit runs every day, on set weekdays or a number of times a week. Habits have streaks, a month grid and a push reminder.
- **Gym:** Plan each workout day and tick each set. A calendar shows Trained and Missed days. Each exercise keeps its history, and a push arrives on gym days.
- **Shopping list:** Tick items at the counter. Check out adds one expense with the total. Items can go into Inventory.
- **Journal:** Write one entry a day with a mood and an optional photo. Past entries are on a calendar.
- **Health:** Track medicines with a reminder per dose, doctor visits with encrypted lab photos, and a weight chart.

### Across the app

- **Fuel log:** Each fill-up writes the odometer and the expense. The Vehicle tab shows km per litre between full tanks and the fuel cost this month.
- **Warranties and receipts:** Inventory items hold the purchase date, price, receipt photo and warranty end. A reminder arrives one month before the warranty ends.
- **Screen time:** Home shows today's total, top apps, past days and a 7-day total from Usage access. This is on Android only, and the data stays on the phone.
- **Daily tip:** A new tip arrives at 12:00 AM PHT from the Gemini free tier, on a random topic. All devices show the same tip. A bundled tip shows if the new tip is missing.
- **Weekly review:** On Sundays, Home and an email show money against budgets, todos, the next service, videos, habits and gym.
- **Email templates:** The daily digest, the Sunday review and the six sign-in emails now use the app look.
- **Brand icons:** The app knows 125 brands. 89 have an icon from Simple Icons, and the others show a letter in the brand colour. The icons are bundled, so the app downloads nothing.
- **Theme:** Pick Same as the phone, Light or Dark. Each device saves its own choice.
- **Face or fingerprint quick unlock:** The Vault offers Off, Fingerprint only or Face or fingerprint. Fingerprint only is the default and the safest. Face or fingerprint is weaker on phones whose face unlock is not Class 3.

### Platform notes

- The Android build is an APK from EAS Build. Install `my-app-1.1.0.apk` over the current app. It is not on the Play Store.
- Screen time needs Usage access on the phone. The web app does not show it.
- Existing subscriptions start with auto-add off, so nothing you logged by hand doubles. Turn it on for each subscription.
