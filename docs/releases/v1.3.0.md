## Release notes

This release is the largest so far. It gives my-app a new look, fixes statement import and the odometer, and adds recurring money, Weather, Steps and sleep, Sports, Find a work and two widgets. It also adds a daily encrypted backup.

### Fixes

- **Statement import:** PDF import works on Android again, for every bank. A server failure no longer asks you to check your connection.
- **Vehicle:** Update odometer accepts a lower number after a confirm step, and a lower reading stops reminders that are no longer true. This changes the old rule that the odometer never goes down.
- **Ulam:** Show another stops at the third dish, and chips go back to any of the three.
- **Inbox:** Play audio shows Loading, the time while it plays, and an error with Try again.
- **Navigation:** Back and Save go to Home when no screen is behind them.

### New features

- **Recurring money:** Expenses and income save themselves on the days you pick, with an Auto label. You can delete a day or pause a rule.
- **Weather:** Pick a place and see the temperature, the rain chance and the forecast, with drawn icons. It is on Home and in More. Your location is optional.
- **Steps and sleep:** Home shows steps from Health Connect and the sleep that you type. Both stay on the phone.
- **Sports:** Matches and standings for five football leagues and the NBA, with crests and team codes. Scores are delayed, and the app shows no live scores.
- **Find a work:** For the admin. The app finds and ranks jobs each day and drafts a cover letter when you ask. You press Submit on the employer page.
- **Widgets:** Two Android home screen widgets show last night's sleep and today's screen time. They show nothing personal after sign-out.
- **Report a bug:** Any account can send a bug or an idea from More. The admin gets a push and reads it on the Reports screen.
- **Reminders:** A tap on a reminder opens its item, not Home.
- **Ulam photos:** Dishes show a real photo that stays on the phone. A dish with no photo keeps its drawing.
- **Journal:** A tap on an entry opens a page to read it.

### Vehicle

- **ADV 160 schedule:** Intervals follow the updated maintenance checker. A custom interval stays. CVT cleaning and steering bearings join the list.
- **Checks:** The before-riding and weekly lists. Ticks stay on the phone, and they clear each day and each Monday.

### New look

- **Banig:** Woven colour, a new font and larger touch targets on every tab and module. Rows replace the information cards.
- **Money:** A chart of this week and a Today list come first. Accounts are swipeable cards. Whole amounts drop ".00".
- **More and Home:** Each module has its icon in its section colour.
- **App icon:** The icon and the favicon are indigo and cream.

### Safety and the repo

- **Database backup:** A daily job saves an encrypted copy of the database for 30 days. Storage files are not in it.
- **RLS check:** `scripts/rls-check.mjs` tests on the live project that one account cannot reach the rows of another. It needs two test accounts.
- **Accounts:** The repo now records that sign-up is off. Only people the admin adds can use the app.
- **Edge Functions:** CI now type checks them.
- **Release workflow:** Each release has five steps, with a record page and a changelog. `README.md` and its banner match this release.

### Platform notes

- The Android build is an APK from Build from GitHub in expo.dev. It stays there, and it is not on this release. Install it over the current app.
- Expo Go does not work with this app, because it needs the dev client and native modules.
- The app can now ask for step access through Health Connect, and for your location when you tap "Use my location".
- The seven database migrations and all nine Edge Functions are already live.
