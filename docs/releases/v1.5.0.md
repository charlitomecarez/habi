## Release notes

This text is a copy of the GitHub release.

v1.5.0 makes the app faster, fixes photos and sheet buttons, and adds sleep and weight from Health Connect, a 6 PM health check, world weather and a vehicle model field. The database moved to Singapore. Install the v1.5.0 APK, because older APKs stop working when the old server pauses.

### Speed

- **Server:** the database moved to Singapore, close to the Philippines, so each request is faster.
- **Screens:** the app keeps your last data on the phone for a week. Home loads in one request instead of about 25. Sign out clears the saved data.
- **Speed check:** a Settings row shows the typical time and the slowest of your last 50 requests, with Fast, Slow or Very slow.
- **CI:** each PR checks the web bundle against a size budget and runs Lighthouse.

### Fixes

- **Photos:** new photos save as WebP with a small list copy. A photo you saw once opens from the phone. Three ulam photos load a smaller copy.
- **Sheets:** Save teams, Save modules and Save settings stay in view above the navigation bar.

### Health

- **Health Connect:** the app reads last night's sleep and your weight from Samsung Health. A typed weight on the same day wins, and a deleted weight does not come back.
- **Sleep:** Steps and sleep shows This week and Last week, with a sleep score and Rested, Fair or Poor.
- **Health check:** 1 to 3 tips each evening at 6 PM. It is off until you turn it on. Turn off deletes the shared data.

### Across the app

- **Weather:** a World section shows up to 6 places with their local time.
- **Vehicle:** Add vehicle starts with a Model field, with the Honda ADV 160 picked.

### What moved

- Milestone 5 (unit coverage gate, E2E and Maestro tests) is not built. It moves to a later release.
- Milestones 12 to 15 moved to v1.6.0 in the plan.

### Platform notes

- The APK comes from Build from GitHub in expo.dev, and it is not on the GitHub release. Install it over v1.4.x.
- The 3 migrations, `health-insight` and `daily-ulam` are live.
- The old Frankfurt project is still active. Pause it when every user has the v1.5.0 APK, and delete it 2 weeks later.

- Sign in on the new APK and see all data.
- Home makes 3 requests or fewer on the web, and a cold start shows Home with no skeleton.
- Photo sizes on the web, and Inventory photos with no second download.
- Save teams with 20 teams, and the Modules, Shortcuts and Ulam settings footers.
- Add an ADV 160 on the web.
- Samsung Health sleep and weight on the phone, This week, and a deleted weight that stays deleted.
