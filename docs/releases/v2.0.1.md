## Concerns

The owner found these problems on the phone with the v2.0.0 APK:

- Back does not work: the back arrow, the phone back button and the drag back.
- When you scroll down, the header number gets very small.
- The E2E job fails in CI.
- The add button shows "+ Add" and then a large gap.
- Some pages do not scroll down.
- Home should show places near you.
- The place button shows only "Add to a".
- Places near you should show a photo when one exists.
- Money should have Today, This month and This year, because one long page is hard to read.
- Screen time says how good your use was, with no clear basis. It should compare with real data.

## Causes and fixes

| Concern                  | Cause                                                                                                                                                                       | Fix                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Back and the E2E job     | In expo-router 58, `usePreventRemove` blocks `navigation.dispatch(data.action)` again. A save or Discard changes ran the blocked back in a loop, and the form never closed. | `useDiscardGuard` uses `repeat()` and the `disablePrevention` function that the hook returns.                                               |
| Pages that do not scroll | The module header got shorter as you scrolled. In a short list, that left nothing to scroll, so the list jumped back to the top and the header grew again.                  | The header shrinks only when the list has at least 200 px to scroll with the large header.                                                  |
| Small header number      | `TabField` scaled the number from 34 px to 20 px.                                                                                                                           | The number stays full size.                                                                                                                 |
| Cut-off labels           | The app loaded its font at run time. Android measured some labels before the font applied, then drew them wider, and cut the last word.                                     | The `expo-font` plugin puts the four Schibsted Grotesk files inside the APK. This needs a new APK. The owner must confirm it on the phone.  |
| Screen time basis        | The status compared today only with your own 7-day average.                                                                                                                 | A 3-hour guide, from adult health guidance of 2 to 3 hours of personal screen time. From 5 hours the status is High, from an OECD analysis. |

Screen time sources: [All About Vision](https://www.allaboutvision.com/eye-care/parents-kids/screen-time-by-age/) gives 2 to 4 hours of recreational screen time for adults, and one health service gives 3 hours or less. No official adult limit exists. The [Digital 2025 report](https://technology.inquirer.net/140462/digital-2025-report-shows-the-latest-global-online-trends) gives 5 h 21 min of phone internet a day in the Philippines.

## Release notes

This text is a copy of the GitHub release.

v2.0.1 fixes the v2.0.0 phone problems, adds places near you on Home, and splits Money into Today, This month and This year. Install the v2.0.1 APK for the label fix.

### Fixes

- **Back:** Back works again after a save and after Discard changes. This also fixes the E2E job.
- **Scrolling:** a short list keeps the large header, so it no longer jumps back to the top.
- **Tab numbers:** the Money, Todos and Vehicle numbers stay full size when you scroll.
- **Labels:** the font is inside the APK, for "Add to a" and the gap after "Add" on the add button. Needs a phone test.

### New

- **Home:** Places near you shows the 3 nearest sights. Home never asks for location.
- **Travel:** a place shows a photo when OpenStreetMap or Wikimedia has one. The button says "Add to trip".
- **Money:** Today, This month and This year. This year shows the year's spending and income, a bar per month and the top categories.
- **Screen time:** a 3-hour guide: Within guide, Above guide, and High from 5 hours. The screen says where the guide comes from.

### What moved

- Plan, design and handoff were skipped.
- The Impeccable audit and critique commands did not run on these changes.

### Platform notes

- Install the v2.0.1 APK from Build from GitHub in expo.dev. It is not on the GitHub release.
- `places-nearby` is deployed for the place photos.
- No migration.
