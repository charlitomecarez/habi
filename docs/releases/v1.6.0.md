## Planning status

The plan has 7 milestones, from `main` at `760edae`. It holds the work that moved out of v1.5.0:

- Milestones 12 to 15 of the v1.5.0 plan: F1 and MotoGP, Movies, YouTube and Travel places. The v1.5.0 plan answers for these stay.
- Milestone 5 of v1.5.0: the unit coverage gate, the E2E tests and the Maestro flows.

The v1.5.0 phone checks have no result yet. The release milestone runs them with the v1.6.0 checks, on one APK.

Charli confirmed the plan on Nov 11, 2026, with the recommended answer to each of the 6 questions. The answers are in the `decisions` collection of the plan page. `TMDB_KEY` and `GEOAPIFY_KEY` are set on the live project.

## Design status

The design covers milestones 2, 3, 4 and 5. Milestones 0, 1 and 6 need no design. The page gives the reason for each.

- Three shape answers: a film opens a detail screen, a place opens a sheet with Open in Maps and Add to a trip, and videos show in one list, newest first.
- One change from the shape answer: the video filter is one Channel field, not chips, because filter chips wrap and a person can follow up to 30 channels.
- Checks: the Impeccable detector and critique ran on the page, and the DB design passed the migration-reviewer checklist. The page lists each finding and the fix.

## Handoff status

Charli confirmed the design on Nov 11, 2026, with no revisions. The handoff has 7 milestones, from `main` at `760edae`.
