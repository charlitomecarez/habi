## Release notes

This text is a copy of the GitHub release.

This release adds Alarms, Ulam and Travel, a new look, and stronger Vault protection.

### New modules

- **Alarms:** A native alarm rings on a locked phone, on the alarm volume, and survives a restart. Alarms can repeat. To stop an alarm, scan the printed label and answer 3 easy sums, or answer 8 sums if the camera fails. Three minutes later, an awake check opens. The sound starts again if nobody taps in 60 seconds.
- **Ulam:** A daily job asks Groq for ulam and stores them. Each ulam shows prices and steps. The app reads only the stored row, and shows a bundled ulam if the row is missing. The module keeps a 7-day log.
- **Travel:** Plan trips with days, ideas, a budget, reminders and Mark as paid. Export PDF makes a day-by-day plan on the phone. The PDF leaves out booking references, notes, ideas and costs unless you turn them on.

### New look

- **Sections:** Each section has its own colour pair, in light and dark themes.
- **Tab bar:** The open tab has a filled icon on a tinted pill. Vehicle has a motorbike icon.
- **Motion:** Motion stops when reduce motion is on.

### Home and More

- **Home:** Home has a Today card, one merged Needs attention list and shortcuts you pick. The list covers todos, service, bills, medicines, debts and birthdays.
- **More:** More is a searchable grid in four sections. Each tile shows how much is inside. The Home photo opens More.
- **Settings:** Settings is at the end of More.

### Vault

- **Master password:** A new vault needs 16 or more characters and refuses common passwords. The app suggests a passphrase.
- **Backups:** Export backup saves the encrypted rows. Restore backup puts them into an empty vault after it proves the master password.
- **Clipboard:** Android marks copied passwords as sensitive. The app clears them from the clipboard, also in the background.
- **Screenshots:** Vault screens and decrypted photos block screenshots.
- **Auto-lock:** The vault locks after 1 minute in the background and 2 minutes idle.

### Docs and tooling

- **Docs:** Nine module docs, and memory, Docker and test rules.
- **Checks:** Commit hooks, CI, new lint rules and parser tests.
